Membership

Welcome to the Converse Flying Eagles! Whether you are interested in joining or are a current member, we are glad you are part of our RC flying community.

Membership Documents

Club dues and fees

Annual club dues help maintain our flying field and equipment. The November 2026 membership application lists these membership options:

$100

$5

Regular membership received after September 30 is good through the following year.

Late-summer membership

These options are for prior-year non-members only:

  • $75: received July 1 through September 30; expires December 31.
  • $25: received October 1 through December 31; expires December 31.

New-member fees and discounts

New members pay a one-time field improvement fee equal to their annual dues. New-member annual dues are prorated by joining date; the field improvement fee is not prorated.

Annual dues are discounted for family members of adult members in the same household and for student members. Contact the club Secretary to confirm your category and total payment.

Renewing your membership

Renewals are due January 1. Memberships follow the calendar year and expire December 31, subject to the regular-membership carryover described above.

A late fee applies to current members for dues received after March 31. March 31 is the late-fee date, not a membership extension or grace period. Contact the Secretary to confirm the late fee due.

No separate renewal form is required. Pay at a club meeting or mail a check to:

Converse Flying Eagles R/C Club
PO Box 214
Converse, IN 46919

Each member is responsible for checking their AMA membership status directly with the AMA.

Membership and joining

Full members are age 19 or older as of March 31 of the application year. Junior members are younger than 19 as of that date. Applicants age 19 or younger as of March 31 need written permission from a parent or legal guardian.

For flying, club members must hold an AMA Full Adult or Full Youth membership in good standing and follow the AMA Safety Code. Junior flying requires supervision by a parent member or a full club member who takes responsibility for the junior member's actions.

If a member loses AMA eligibility, club flying privileges end until that eligibility is restored.

Print and complete the application, and read and agree to the bylaws and field rules. New members normally pay at their first club meeting. Bring cash or a check if paying in person, or mail the completed application with a check to the club address above.

Your membership cannot become active until the club has your completed application and dues payment and has verified your AMA status. Contact the Secretary to arrange submission and the required approval steps.

Lifetime membership is awarded by club vote and includes full privileges and voting rights. AMA membership is required for flying.

Your membership card

The club provides membership cards. After processing, the Secretary emails a PDF card to print, cut out, and fold in half. ID badge holders are available at the field. If you would like a card mailed to you, include a self-addressed stamped envelope.

Membership and document questions

Contact the club Secretary at LESouthwick@outlook.com with questions about membership, the bylaws, or field rules.

Flying safely and using the field

Field access is restricted to AMA members and sponsored guests. Follow the AMA and club safety rules posted on the information board in the pavilion.

  • Do not fly over buildings, cars, spectators, or the pit area.
  • Call “On the field” before proceeding onto the runway.
  • Keep motor vehicles off the grass runway.
  • Spectators must stay behind the chain-link fence.

New members must demonstrate positive control of their aircraft before being released to fly solo. The club provides free instruction to anyone who requests it or needs remedial help. Visit Learning to Fly to get started.

Pilots are responsible for FAA compliance, including registration and labeling where required and completion of TRUST for recreational flying. Fixed-wing models are also subject to FAA requirements. The club does not enforce FAA registration rules; use the FAA registration guidance for current requirements.

Club business and safety concerns

The Board of Directors determines membership types and limits. Officers are elected in November and installed in December, with terms running January 1 through December 31. General club business is decided by a majority of members present when at least three officers are present. Bylaw amendments require a proposed motion at a general meeting and majority approval.

The Safety Committee is the Board of Directors chaired by the Safety Officer. Serious or unresolved safety complaints use the grievance form included in the bylaws and require at least one witness signature. The written grievance procedure discloses complainants' names. Contact the Safety Officer at a meeting about safety protocols.
