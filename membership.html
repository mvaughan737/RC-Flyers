Membership

Welcome to the Converse Flying Eagles! Whether you are interested in joining or are a current member, we are glad you are part of our RC flying community.

Membership Documents

Joining, renewing, and dues

Annual club dues help maintain our flying field and equipment. The November 2026 membership application lists these membership options:

Regular membership
$100
Spouse membership
$5
Junior membership Under 19 as of March 31
No charge

Club members must maintain an active Academy of Model Aeronautics (AMA) membership to fly.

Interested in joining?

Print and complete the membership application below. Read the bylaws and field rules, then contact the club to confirm how to submit your application and payment, and which dues apply to your joining date.

Already a member?

Renewal payments can be made at club meetings or mailed to the club address below. Contact the club to confirm the amount due, any applicable late fee, and the paperwork needed.

Converse Flying Eagles R/C Club
PO Box 214
Converse, IN 46919
