Learning to Fly

Starting out in RC aviation is one of the most rewarding experiences you can have. At Converse Flying Eagles, we are committed to helping beginners take flight safely and confidently.

Getting Started

New pilots are encouraged to talk with club members before buying equipment or attempting a first flight. We can explain the basics, answer questions, and help you understand what to expect as you begin learning to fly radio-controlled aircraft.

Training Aircraft & Gear

Do not buy your first airplane alone. The club has designated trainer aircraft for instruction, and instructors may also have trainer aircraft and compatible controllers available. When you are ready to purchase your own equipment, we can help you choose a beginner-friendly aircraft and radio system.

How Instructor Controls Work

Training is commonly done with a buddy-box style setup. The instructor remains in control of the aircraft and can pass control to the student when it is safe to practice. If the aircraft gets out of position or the student needs help, the instructor can instantly take control back.

Different radio systems handle this in different ways. Some use a trainer or bind button, while others use programmable master override features. The goal is the same: give the student real stick time while keeping the flight safe.

How to Get Started

  1. Contact the Club: Reach out to a member or send a message through the Contact Form so we can help you plan your first visit.
  2. Visit the Field: Come watch, ask questions, and see what RC flying looks like in person.
  3. Connect with an Instructor: After you become a member, the club can help connect or sign you up with an instructor if requested.

Visiting the Field

There are not guaranteed designated times when members will be at the field unless a flying activity or club event is published on the Website Events page. Members often fly in the mornings and late afternoons when winds are usually lighter, but activity varies with weather, schedules, and field conditions.

The best first step is to contact a member or send a message through the Contact Form. The club will respond with the information you need to get started and help you make a good first visit.

After You Join

Once you become a member, we can help connect you with an instructor if you would like training support. Instruction is intended to help new pilots build confidence, learn safe habits, and progress toward flying independently.
