About the Converse Flying Eagles

The Converse Flying Eagles RC Club is a community of aviation enthusiasts dedicated to the hobby and sport of radio-controlled flight. Based at the Converse Airport in Converse, Indiana, we provide a safe and welcoming environment for pilots of all skill levels.

Field Facilities & Amenities

The Converse Flying Eagles RC Club is proud to offer a well-maintained flying site designed to provide a safe, comfortable, and enjoyable experience for pilots, members, families, and visitors. Our field features a well-maintained grass runway and a spacious grass pit area, providing ample room for a wide variety of radio-controlled aircraft operations.

For convenience and comfort, the club offers covered shelters, setup tables, workbenches, startup stands, electrical power, and battery charging capabilities. Pilots can take advantage of our windsock poles and windsocks, as well as an on-site weather station to help monitor flying conditions throughout the day.

Safety remains a top priority. The field is equipped with safety fencing, fire extinguishers, a first aid kit, and sandbags for responding to battery incidents and potential lithium battery thermal runaway events, helping ensure a safe environment for all participants and spectators. The facility is handicap accessible and includes designated spectator viewing areas where visitors are always welcome to watch the action. Dedicated bleacher seating is available during club events, fly-ins, and contests, providing comfortable viewing areas for members, families, guests, and spectators.

Additional amenities include portable restroom facilities, camping opportunities during special events, and support for night flying activities when conditions permit. During the winter months and periods of inclement weather, members gather in the club hangar for monthly meetings, fellowship, planning sessions, and educational programs.

Whether you're an experienced RC pilot, a newcomer to the hobby, or simply interested in watching model aviation in action, the Converse Flying Eagles RC Club offers one of the most complete and welcoming flying facilities in the region.

Our Philosophy

We believe that the best way to build a strong club is through personal connection. Unlike many modern organizations, we prefer to meet prospective members in person at our field or during club meetings before formalizing membership. This ensures a great fit for both the individual and our community.

✈️ Ready to Join the Converse Flying Eagles?

Whether you're an experienced RC pilot or just discovering the hobby, we'd love to welcome you to our club. The Converse Flying Eagles offer a friendly environment where members can fly, learn, share experiences, and enjoy the excitement of radio-controlled aviation.

Getting Started Is Easy

  • ✅ Visit Our Flying Field
    Check our Events Calendar and stop by during a scheduled event to experience RC flying firsthand.
  • ✅ Attend a Club Meeting
    Join us on the first Tuesday of each month at 6:00 PM to meet members, learn about the club, and ask questions.
  • ✅ Meet Our Members
    Our members are always happy to talk about aircraft, flying techniques, and getting started in the hobby.
  • ✅ Complete Your Membership Application
    Submit our Contact Form and attend a club meeting to complete your membership paperwork.
  • ✅ Become an AMA Member
    Membership in the Academy of Model Aeronautics (AMA) is required for club membership. We'll be glad to help guide you through the process.

We look forward to meeting you and sharing the fun of RC aviation!

FAA Recognized Identification Area (FRIA)

Our flying field is an FAA Recognized Identification Area (FRIA). This means our members can fly their RC aircraft in compliance with federal regulations, ensuring a safe and legal environment for everyone involved.
