All pilots flying at the Converse Flying Eagles RC Club must maintain a current membership with the Academy of Model Aeronautics (AMA).

AMA membership provides valuable liability insurance, promotes safe model aviation, and supports clubs and flying sites across the United States. It is also a requirement for flying at our AMA-chartered field.

Current annual AMA membership rates are:

  • Adult, ages 19–64: $89 per year
  • Senior, age 65 and older: $79 per year
  • Youth, under age 19: $20 per year

For the latest membership information or to join the AMA, visit the Academy of Model Aeronautics website.

The FAA requires all recreational RC pilots to successfully complete The Recreational UAS Safety Test (TRUST) before flying.

The TRUST course is free, takes only a short time to complete, and provides a completion certificate that must be available whenever you fly.

The TRUST Certificate is a one-time requirement and does not expire. Once you have successfully completed the course, you do not need to take it again.

If you have not yet completed the TRUST course, our club members will be happy to point you toward an FAA-approved TRUST testing provider.

FAA Reference: The TRUST requirement is established under 49 U.S.C. § 44809, “Exception for Limited Recreational Operations of Unmanned Aircraft,” which requires recreational flyers to pass the FAA’s aeronautical knowledge and safety test and carry proof of completion while flying.

It depends on the weight of your aircraft.

Most recreational aircraft weighing more than 250 grams, or 0.55 pounds, must be registered with the FAA and display a valid FAA registration number on the aircraft.

Aircraft weighing 250 grams, or 0.55 pounds, or less may not require FAA registration when flown recreationally, provided they have not been modified to exceed the weight threshold.

Unlike the TRUST Certificate, FAA aircraft registration must be renewed every three years for aircraft that require registration.

If you are unsure whether your aircraft needs to be registered, our experienced club members will be happy to help you determine the current FAA requirements.

Remote ID is an FAA requirement that allows certain aircraft to broadcast identification and location information while flying.

Many newer aircraft already include Standard Remote ID. Aircraft that do not may require a Remote ID broadcast module unless they are operated at an FAA-Recognized Identification Area (FRIA), such as our club flying site.

If you have questions about whether your aircraft requires Remote ID, our experienced members will be happy to help.
